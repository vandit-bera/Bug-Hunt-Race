# Sound effects

All six sounds (`tick`, `go`, `pass`, `fail`, `solved`, `timeup`) are
synthesized from plain sine and square tones by `scripts/generate-sounds.mjs`
in this repository. They contain no third-party samples. They are original
work released under [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).

Regenerate with `node scripts/generate-sounds.mjs`.
